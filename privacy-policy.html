Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Fruta Confidencial Stats is a private analytics tool used by the owner of Fruta Confidencial to retrieve and organize performance statistics from their own TikTok account.

The application may access basic TikTok profile information and video performance data, including video titles, publication dates, views, likes, comments and shares. This information is used exclusively to display statistics in a private Google Sheets dashboard.

The application does not sell, share or disclose personal data to third parties. Access credentials and retrieved data are used only by the account owner and are not made publicly available.

Users may revoke the application's access at any time through their TikTok account settings. To request access to, correction of or deletion of data, contact: jorgeperezmingote7@gmail.com.
